# VP Engineering Review

The VP Engineering Review is the final checkpoint before implementation. The VP Engineering is the most experienced, thoughtful leader who provides the final go-no-go decision. The goal is to ensure that the work is well-designed, secure, performant, and maintainable before it goes into production.
